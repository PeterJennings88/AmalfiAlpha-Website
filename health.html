The Partnership

Premium virtual healthcare. Nationwide.

Velocity Health is the exclusive concierge medicine partner of AmalfiAlpha. Their model is built on something rare in modern healthcare: real conversations with real physicians, not automated reports.

Every member receives extended quarterly consultations, comprehensive diagnostic panels, and ongoing access to a multidisciplinary clinical team — all designed around how high performers actually want to manage their health.

  • i.Quarterly 90-minute physician visits. Time to actually go deep, not the 10 minutes you get from a typical practice.
  • ii.Comprehensive diagnostics. Bloodwork, hormone panels, gut health, and advanced biomarker testing — coordinated and explained.
  • iii.A full clinical team. Physicians, dietitians, behavioral-health specialists, and longevity-focused care.
  • iv.Personalized health plans. Built around your performance goals, not generic guidelines.
