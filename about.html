Our Story

Built for the people who refuse to coast

AmalfiAlpha began as a private network of accredited investors, entrepreneurs, and former professional athletes who shared one thing in common: they wanted to keep growing — in their wealth, their health, and their craft — and they wanted to do it alongside people who were doing the same.

What started as a community has evolved into a four-pillar membership: institutional-quality investment deal flow, concierge medicine through Velocity Health, an evolving AI Academy, and the in-person experiences that turn networks into real friendships.

Every part of AmalfiAlpha exists to compound — not just your portfolio, but your knowledge, your health, and the people you can call when it matters.

  • i.Camaraderie. A network of high performers who genuinely show up for each other.
  • ii.Competition. Friendly, frequent, and the best forcing function for growth.
  • iii.Compounding. In wealth, in health, in knowledge, and in relationships.
