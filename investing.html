The Approach

Deal flow with conviction behind it

The hardest part of investing isn't finding deals. It's knowing which deals are real — and which look great on a deck but will quietly waste a year of your time and capital.

AmalfiAlpha solves this by routing every member-shared opportunity through an Investment Committee of operators and investors who have lived the outcomes. The deals you see in the portal have already passed a peer review by people whose own capital is in the game.

  • i.Member-sourced. Members surface the deals they're personally backing — not deck spam.
  • ii.Committee-vetted. Each opportunity is reviewed for thesis, terms, and integrity before being shared.
  • iii.Transparently presented. Memos include the why, the risks, and who else is in.
  • iv.Discussed openly. The community debates each deal before anyone commits.
