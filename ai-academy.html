AI Academy

The leverage you'd be crazy not to use

Practical AI training for investors and operators — taught live, refined every quarter, and built around the tools that actually move the needle. Available exclusively to AmalfiAlpha members.

Why It Matters

The gap is widening

The members who know how to use AI well are pulling ahead of the ones who don't — in research depth, in operating leverage, and in the speed at which they can pursue opportunities.

The AmalfiAlpha AI Academy exists because the cost of falling behind compounds fast. Members get a direct path from "I've heard about it" to "I'm using it every day to make better decisions."

  • i.Practical, not theoretical. Every session ends with something you can use the next morning.
  • ii.Live and recorded. Attend live workshops or watch on demand inside the member portal.
  • iii.Always current. The curriculum updates as the tools do — not once a year.
  • iv.Member-led. Real members teaching what's actually working in their businesses.

The Curriculum

Three tracks. One trajectory.

Track 01

AI for Investors

Use AI to research deals faster, analyze public companies in depth, and pressure-test theses before you write a check.

  • Reading 10-Ks at 10× speed
  • LLM-powered earnings analysis
  • Building a private deal-screening pipeline
  • Sourcing and qualifying private rounds with AI
  • Risk and red-flag detection

Track 02

AI for Operators

Apply AI inside your business — automate the busy work, build internal tools without engineers, and free your team to focus on what compounds.

  • Building internal tools without code
  • AI-powered workflow automation
  • Sales and customer intelligence pipelines
  • Document, contract, and meeting automation
  • Hiring and managing AI inside your org

Track 03

AI Foundations

For members starting from zero — a fast, structured intro to the tools, terminology, and habits that make everything else possible.

  • Choosing the right tools (and ignoring the rest)
  • Prompting like a power user
  • Working with documents, data, and images
  • Building good AI habits
  • Privacy, security, and what to never share

Live workshops. Recorded library.

Members can attend AI Academy sessions live as part of their week, or catch up on demand inside the member portal. Every session is taught by someone with operating skin in the game.
